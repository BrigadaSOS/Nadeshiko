Hi! I'm David, one of the creators of Nadeshiko. Thanks for signing up. I wanted to walk you through what the site does in roughly the order you will run into it, so you know what is waiting for you before you go looking for it.

Search a word, hear it in a real scene

Start with a word you half-know and put it in the search box in Japanese, or in English or Spanish if the Japanese has not stuck yet. What comes back are real lines out of anime and J-drama and YouTube with the audio exactly as it was said, so you get to hear how the word actually lands in a sentence rather than how a dictionary says it should.

{{#stepOneImage}} {{/stepOneImage}}

Click any word for its reading and meaning

Any word in a line can be clicked and its readings and meanings open right there on top of the sentence you were already reading. It sounds like a small thing until you notice you have stopped losing your place every time you need to check something.

{{#stepTwoImage}} {{/stepTwoImage}}

Replay the line until you can say it

Every result comes with the clip it was taken from and you can play it back as many times as you need until the rhythm of it sticks. This is the part people use for shadowing, because you are copying a delivery that actually happened instead of guessing at one from the text.

{{#stepThreeImage}} {{/stepThreeImage}}

Keep what you find

This is what the account is actually for, and it is where most people end up spending their time.

  • Save anything you want to come back to into a collection and it will still be sitting there whenever you get round to it.
  • Send a line straight to Anki in one click and the sentence and its audio and its screenshot and the definition all arrive on the card together.
  • Point it at your own deck and note type once and every export after that just follows what you set.
