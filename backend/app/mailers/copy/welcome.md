## search

### Search a word and hear it in a real scene

Put any Japanese or English word in the search box and we will show you real lines from anime, movies, J-drama and YouTube channels. Play the audio to hear the exact context it is used in. You can also download the screenshot and the audio, or send them straight to Anki to finish your cards.

## wordCard

### Click a word, see its definition

We have built our own dictionary into Nadeshiko. Whether you are on desktop or mobile, clicking any word in a sentence opens a window with the English definition, readings, pitch accent and kanji breakdown. No need to jump to another app to look a word up, it's all right here!

## player

### Replay the line until you can say it

Switch to player mode and you can play a line back as many times as you want. Useful for shadowing along with the shows you love and improving your pronunciation.
